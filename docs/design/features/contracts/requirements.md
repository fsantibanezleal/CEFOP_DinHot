# Requirements: contracts, providers and declared states (ADR-0080)

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The contracts are the rows of [`docs/contracts/catalog.md`](../../../contracts/catalog.md); the states and their reasons are its section Missing-data states. DinHot's catalog has twelve rows: the params record (contract 1), the case index, the case manifest, the trace (contract 2), the benchmark results, the two model slots (`cgh-net`, `warmstart-model`), the model metrics, the engine reference, the readiness and the dormant API. Rows CT-034 to CT-038 are the template's guards of the catalog and of the repository, held as they are. The two model slots keep a baseline behind the same contract (rows CT-012 to CT-018).
