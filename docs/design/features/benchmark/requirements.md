# Requirements: the benchmark: protocol, fairness and skipped cells

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The benchmark (`data-pipeline/pipeline/benchmark/protocol.py`, document `data/derived/benchmark/results.json`) runs every method valid for a
geometry, with the same seeds, the same metrics and the same budgets, on the geometries G1 to G7: medians and inter-quartile ranges over the
seeds, one row per method, geometry, device, budget and parameter set, and the points of the B5 efficiency-uniformity continuation. Its numbers are DinHot's own and measured, never
quoted. Budgets: the 10, 30 and 100 iteration rows are runs of exactly that length, the 50 ms and 1 s rows are time-capped runs, and one
converged run per seed serves as the 60 s row; every run is scored from its final phase through the device. Tuned rows exist only for
methods that declare a tuning grid. A cell that cannot run is listed with a bilingual reason and is absent from the rows. The release run
(20 seeds, 256 pixels) is a workstation job, never CI (ADR-0074); the tests run the same code on the reduced grid (64 pixels, 2 seeds) with
planted methods (`tests/bake/planted.py`).

| BM-009 | WHEN a method has a time budget, THE warm start and every later phase of B1 (warm start, outer loop, every evaluation of a CG line search), B2 (frame 0, every frame iteration) and B5 (warm start, every lambda and its line searches) SHALL stop at the deadline, overrunning by at most one unit of work, and SHALL keep the best iterate reached. | `tests/methods/beyond/test_equal_time.py::test_b1_stops_warm_start_and_cg_at_its_deadline_and_keeps_the_best_iterate` |

| BM-011 | THE static benchmark SHALL not run B2, the flicker-bounded sequence (its frame 0 is the phase-fixed WGS it would be ranked against), and the protocol SHALL say why. | `tests/bake/test_benchmark_equal_time.py::test_no_static_row_for_b2_and_the_two_g6_targets_are_separate` |
| BM-012 | THE time and converged rows SHALL run at least 5 seeds for every method, geometry and device whatever the group has spent, and THE iteration rows SHALL keep the 600 s group budget with at least 2 seeds. | `tests/bake/test_benchmark_equal_time.py::test_seed_rule_time_and_converged_rows_run_five_seeds_iteration_rows_keep_the_group_budget` |
| BM-013 | THE large-array geometry SHALL be two geometries, G6a (lattice 32 x 32) and G6b (disordered 819), each one target on every seed, so no row mixes the two. | `tests/bake/test_benchmark_equal_time.py::test_g6_is_two_geometries_with_their_own_targets_and_every_seed_runs_both` |

| BM-015 | THE best published and the best proposed method of a cell SHALL be read as a win only if both have at least 5 seeds, their interquartile ranges of the relative standard deviation do not overlap and their medians differ by at least 0.01 percentage points; otherwise the cell SHALL be a tie, and an iteration row SHALL never produce a win; the report's extract scripts and the app SHALL apply the same rule on the same fixture; a time row over its budget is not read (BM-017). | `tests/test_benchmark_win_rule.py::test_cell_result_on_the_shared_fixture` |

| BM-018 | EVERY worker process of the benchmark's pool, and the calling process of its sequential path, SHALL opt out of the operating system's power throttling where the system has it (Windows EcoQoS), and the protocol text SHALL say so in English and Spanish; the opt-out SHALL never raise and SHALL do nothing on another system. | `tests/engine/test_power.py::test_on_windows_the_opt_out_is_set_and_read_back`, `tests/engine/test_power.py::test_the_benchmark_pool_is_built_with_the_initializer`, `tests/engine/test_power.py::test_the_sequential_benchmark_opts_out_in_the_calling_process` |
