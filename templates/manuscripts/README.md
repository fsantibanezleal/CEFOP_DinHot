# Native manuscript sources

These are **incomplete, unpublished formatting blueprints**, not reports of the template's teaching example. A product authors two separate documents: its complete technical report and the research paper for each proposed method or other novel contribution. The paper uses sections, never chapters. The shared presentation is derived from the authoritative CAOS Research LaTeX base; [provenance](provenance.json) records the source identities and the two explicit draft-identity adaptations. These attributed manuscript sources retain CC BY 4.0; the rest of the template's software remains under its repository license.

Copy the selected folder into `manuscripts/<slug>/` and copy `shared/base.tex` into `<product root>/manuscripts/shared/base.tex`. The relative input in `main.tex` then resolves without a machine path. Keep each document's native source, bibliography, generated vector figures, source-generating scripts and `coverage.json` in the product repository. The author identity in these blueprints is Felipe Santibáñez-Leal's existing public manuscript identity; an instantiated product must confirm its actual authors and contributions before publication.

Replace all instructional text. Derive the title and abstract from the actual methods, scope and results. Populate the bibliography with verified cited primary sources, enable the bibliography commands and generate numerical tables and figures from the actual product artifacts. Empty DOI macros are intentionally printed as unassigned and produce no DOI link. Never invent identifiers or borrow the example studies' DOIs. The publication workflow reserves the actual version DOI only after content acceptance, patches the identity macros and rebuilds the final PDF.

Build from the document's folder with `latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -outdir=<external-build-directory> main.tex`. A manual build uses `pdflatex`, BibTeX and two further `pdflatex` passes after adding real citations. The unfilled formatting proof has no bibliography entries and needs only two `pdflatex` passes. Keep generated build outputs outside the source checkout. Review every rendered page, not only the compiler exit code: equations, notation, captions, tables, running headers, links, source attribution and complete figures all matter.

Run `python scripts/check_manuscript_depth.py --report` and the existing metadata/voice/publication checks before release. A copied, unfilled blueprint must fail the authored-content check. The mechanical floors are 12,000/7,000 prose words, 25/12 display equations, 10/6 figures, 8/4 tables, 30/25 cited references and 3/1 algorithms for report/paper. They supplement the full required structure, section depth, method/contract/contribution coverage and independent scientific/content/visual review. Counts do not establish novelty or correctness.

The frozen [historical source examples](../../tests/fixtures/manuscript-depth/README.md) show complete native source structure and preserve two negative SoftQP studies. They are test/style evidence only, not new findings, training results or current product qualification.
