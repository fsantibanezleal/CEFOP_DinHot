## Findings for the main session

- `product.json` `method_entrypoints` lacks the learned and volumetric methods: `m17_cgh_net.run`, `m17_cgh_net.predict`,
  `m17_cgh_net.planes_from_traps`, `m19_multiplane.run`, `b3_warm_start.run`, `learned.evaluation.plane_accuracy`, `learned.evaluation.volume_target`
  and `learned.generators.sample_volume`. The gate counts a method cell only for a declared entry point, so the notebooks of topic 08 reach their
  floor through calls of `core.forward`, `core.modes`, `core.traps`, `core.metrics` and M04. Declaring those functions would let the lessons count
  the calls that carry them.
- The runner's `--check` does not copy `models/` (base defect BD-2, fixed in template 0.13.001): the notebooks of topic 08 (this unit's 01 and 02,
  and U12c's 03) read `models/cgh-net.onnx`, so their `--check` run is PENDING the adoption of 0.13.001. Their committed outputs were written by
  the in-place runner (`run_notebooks.py --only <notebook>`), which sees the repository.
- The engine's own start of M08 (a Fresnel lens of 20 waves) scores an error of 0.0322 on the C04 disc with 0.0887 of the power in the signal region,
  lower than the 0.0517 of M08 after 30 iterations (power 0.1719): the accuracy that `04-shaped-potentials/03` credits to MRAF is measured against
  M04, not against its own start, and the reviewer of that notebook may want the sentence qualified (new 04-01 prints both numbers).
- MRAF with a signal window of half-width 256 on the 512 by 512 grid (no noise region) reproduces M04 to 4.84e-10 rad in the phase: mixing is a
  constant factor of the target when nothing is left free (`04-shaped-potentials/02`).
- On the fresh generated volumes of `08-learned-holography/02` the network has the higher accuracy (median 0.690 against 0.606) and the lower trap
  efficiency (0.388 against 0.745) and uniformity (0.558 against 0.803) than M04 at 30 iterations; the label-free loss does not contain the
  traps' own numbers.
