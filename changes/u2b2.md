## SDD changes

- Section "App", live lane: a change of a control (method, device, iterations, backend, an edited trap, the aberration) submits one run to the
  engine worker after a 200 ms pause with no further change; the next change or the Cancel button stops the run in flight (the worker stops
  within one iteration) and its late answer is dropped, so an old result never replaces a newer one or carries the wrong label. Nothing runs
  on a timer: a run is started only by a change. The rule is `frontend/src/workbench/scheduler.ts`, held by `scheduler.test.ts` over fake timers
  and over the real engine client with a loopback in place of the worker.
- Section "App", backend: the rail's Run section says which backend computed the picture on screen (CPU or WebGPU) and, when WebGPU is not in use,
  the first clause of the engine's reason; the full reason is the title of the line and the hint of the WebGPU chip. The browser's WebGPU probe
  (`client.gpu()`) runs once when the engine is up. The cards no longer repeat the fallback sentence (it cost every drawing a line).
- Section "App", methods: the chip of a method carries its id, the full name is in the note under the group (twelve methods with names did not
  fit the rail, ADR-0071 rule 6). A method the engine can run is live; any other is replayed from the bake with its own metrics and per-trap
  intensities, and the card says the maps drawn are the reference method's.
- Section "Views", V5: the axial cut has two planes. The xz cut of the bake is replayed while the selected trap is the one the bake cut; every
  other cut (the yz plane, another trap, a live hologram, edits) is computed by `client.axialCut` on the hologram on screen with the rail's device,
  trap list and aberration (`paramsFor`). The yz cut is the engine's xz cut of the transposed problem (`cuts.ts`: the phase transposed, every trap's x
  and y exchanged, the aberration added before the transposition); it is exact for a square SLM and an x-y symmetric beam, and
  `cuts.test.ts` holds it equal to the engine's own yz cut (`forward.axialCut` with x given) to 1e-6 of the peak.
- Section "Views", V6: for a three-dimensional case with the engine present the view is the focal volume, not a model of the lobes: the voxels of
  the engine's axial cuts through every row (xz) and every column (yz) of traps, at most six planes per direction, drawn as a point cloud above a
  fraction of the peak (`volume.ts`, `Volume3D.tsx`), with a threshold control, orbit by drag or keys, zoom, reset, rendered on demand. Between the
  planes nothing is drawn, and the card says so. Without the engine the round 1 model of the lobes remains, labelled as a model.
- Section "Views", V10: the particle runs in the exact local map (`localMapFromPhase`, from the phase and the case) of the hologram on screen,
  at the rail's seed and power. The baked trajectory is replayed only while the rail asks for what the bake simulated (its seed, its power, its
  baked hologram, no edits: `atTheBake`); any other setting asks the engine, one simulation per distinct request shared by the particle and the
  spectrum views.
- Section "Views", shaped targets: the Per-trap intensity and Depth views draw the focal intensity over its mean inside the shape (depth is
  proportional to intensity), because a shaped target has no trap list.
- Section "Views", more than 12 traps: the per-trap intensity and depth views draw a sorted points chart (a bar chart of more than 12 bars puts its
  id labels on top of each other, G10).
- Section "Resources": the focal volume computes up to twelve cuts of 64 depth samples one after another in the worker (about one second each on
  the 256 x 256 case C05 on this laptop's CPU); the card shows "Computing plane k of n". The point cloud is capped at 60 000 points.
- Section "Build", copy-data: a declared product file (`models/metrics.json`) that is absent builds when the readiness row says `provider` or
  `missing`, because the row's provider serves it until the producer (M1) exists; any other state stops the build.

- Section "Learned models" (after the merge of M1): cgh-net takes three planes. The input is `[1, 3, 128, 128]`: for the planes -8, 0 and +8
  SLM-limited Rayleigh ranges (z_R = lambda f^2 / (pi R^2), R = N p / 2), the square root of the unit-sum intensity of Gaussian spots of one bin
  at the rounded trap bins (`ort.ts` `encodeCghPlanes`, the Python `planes_from_traps`); it applies only to a 128 x 128 SLM with padding 1 and traps
  on the three planes within 60 bins and with no vortex, and says why when it does not (the baseline answers: M19 at 30 iterations, as the Python
  slot does). No case of the bake has a 128 x 128 SLM, so the released model does not run on any of them; the real graph was run through
  onnxruntime-web in Node (`[1, 3, 128, 128]` in, `[1, 1, 128, 128]` out, 142 ms, finite phase), UNVERIFIED in the browser for want of a fitting case.
  The warm start has no model (B3 refuted): the slot answers with its baseline and the readiness reason says so.
- Readiness rule: a `training` row with provenance `baseline` whose reason says to bake again to use the released model means the released model
  answers (the card is on the site); the row whose reason says "No released model" means the baseline answers and the card is not asked for.
