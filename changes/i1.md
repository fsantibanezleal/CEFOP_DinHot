## Contract changes proposed (ADR-0080: through a version and a History row, by the main session)

The bake could not give the shaped-target metrics their own names without editing `docs/contracts/schemas/`, which unit I1 may not touch. What the
bake writes now is documented in the SDD, section Sizing and contracts (the two required slots carry the signal-region metrics, and the manifest
flags say so). Proposed:

- case-manifest 1.1.0 and trace 1.1.0 (minor, optional fields): in `methods.<id>` (manifest) and `methods.<id>.final` and `history[]` (trace) the
  optional numbers `signal_rms_error_pct` and `signal_efficiency`, and `uniformity` and `rel_std_pct` no longer required when the case target is
  shaped. History row proposed: "1.1.0 | shaped-target metrics named, 2026-10-28". The frontend would then read the named fields and stop labelling
  the carriers as trap metrics.
