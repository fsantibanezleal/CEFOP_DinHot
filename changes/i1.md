# I1 fragment (the integration of the bake with the methods; for the main session to merge into the SDD, the catalog and the changelog)

## Contract changes proposed (ADR-0080: through a version and a History row, by the main session)

The bake could not be given the shaped-target metrics their own names without editing `docs/contracts/schemas/`, which this unit may not
touch. What the bake writes now, and what is proposed:

- Written now (contracts 1.0.0 unchanged): for a shaped target the two required slots carry the signal-region metrics. In the manifest's
  `methods.<id>` and the trace's `methods.<id>.final` and `history[]`: `rel_std_pct` is `signal_rms_error_pct` (the RMS fractional error
  over the measure region after the scale fit, Pasienski and DeMarco 2008, Eq. 2; percent; clipped at 1000, the units domain) and
  `uniformity` is `max(0, 1 - signal_rms_error_pct / 100)`. `efficiency` is the power inside the shape, `zero_order` the zero-order
  fraction, `ghost` the power outside the signal region. The manifest `flags` of every shaped case carry one entry that says so and lists,
  per method, `signal_rms_error_pct` and `signal_efficiency` unclipped. The `summary` text of a shaped case says "signal-region RMS error".
  The `expect` ranges of a shaped case are keyed `<method>.signal_rms_error_pct` and `<method>.signal_efficiency` (new keys of
  `core/expect.py`, which contract 1 imports; the params-record schema's `expect` is free-form, so it accepts them).
- Proposed, case-manifest 1.1.0 and trace 1.1.0 (minor, optional fields): in `methods.<id>` (manifest) and `methods.<id>.final` and
  `history[]` (trace) the optional numbers `signal_rms_error_pct` and `signal_efficiency`, and `uniformity` and `rel_std_pct` no longer
  required when the case target is shaped (or documented as the signal-region carriers above). History row proposed: "1.1.0 | shaped-target
  metrics named, 2026-10-28". The frontend would then read the named fields and stop labelling the carriers as trap metrics.

## Facts for the SDD

- Form selection (F1): M04, M05, M06 and M07 take `form="auto"` by default: the FFT form when every trap is on an FFT bin (to 0.002 bin;
  case coordinates are rounded to 1e-4 um, 1e-4 bin) with z = 0 and charge 0, the point-trap form otherwise. The chosen form is
  `Result.meta["form"]`. M16 calls M07 with `auto`. M12 stays FFT-only: its camera model reads the focal field at the nearest bin, so
  its cases are on bins (C01, C02, C03).
- Case inputs at the boundary (F5): the case's wavefront reaches M14 as `injected` (the system it senses through the camera) and no longer
  B4, whose `aberration` keyword is the rms of residual aberrations (a float); M16 receives the loaded sites as `atoms` and the
  `rearrangement.target_ids` as its target. M16 is scored over all the case's sites (it lights the target sites only), so its uniformity is
  over lit and unlit sites; the C10 ranges do not use it.
- Cases on bins (F8): C01, C02 and C03 are on integer-bin lattices (8, 8 and 10 bins: 3.984, 3.984 and 4.98 um at the atom setup), so the
  FFT form applies and the live lane keeps its 1500 ms budget (the TypeScript engine measured 1.0 to 3.5 s in Chromium for 1024 off-bin
  traps in the point-trap form against 0.49 to 0.80 s for on-bin C02). C05, C06, C07, C08, C09 and C10 keep off-bin traps (3D, vortex,
  high NA, device, aberration, rearrangement). The card note of C03 says so.
- Atom driver (F4): the record's `dt_s` is the sampling step of the trace; the integration step is `dt_s / k` with `k` such that
  `omega_r dt` is at most 0.2 (atom) or `dt` at most `tau / 20` (bead); `k` is at most 64. The atom of the first bake left the field because
  the reference had failed and a superposition hologram put a far deeper trap at the site (`omega dt` above 2, velocity Verlet unstable).
  The units were read against `core/trapphys.py` and `core/dynamics.py` and are consistent. The oracle for an atom is the spectral peak of
  the simulated position against `omega_r = sqrt(U |d2 I_hat/dx2| / m)` of the same map, within 20 percent (not a Lorentzian corner). The
  driver raises when the trajectory leaves the computed map.
- Benchmark G6 (F7): the benchmark schema holds one geometry per id, so G6 pools two targets by seed parity as G7 pools four shapes: even
  seeds the 32 x 32 lattice, odd seeds the disordered 819-trap set of Kim et al. 2019 (`cases.library.disordered_set`, traps on integer bins
  at least 5 bins apart in a +-100 bin window, seeded; the coordinates of the paper are not published). C03's second target variant is NOT a
  case record (a record holds one target and the SDD fixes twelve cases): see the open question in the report.
- Tuning grid (F7): M05's fallback tuning key is `alpha` (it was `gamma`, which `m05_gaa.run` does not take, so M05 was silently untuned).
- Speed (F6): the cases bake in parallel, one case per process, at most 12 processes, one BLAS thread each; methods within a case run in
  sequence. The measured wall time and peak memory of the canonical bake are in the Resources section below.
