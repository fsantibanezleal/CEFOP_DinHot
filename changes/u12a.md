through the pipeline modules of `product.json` `method_entrypoints` (DinHot publishes no package), on cases of
`data/examples/cases.json`, in under three minutes on the development machine. The template's example notebooks
(`01-getting-started`, `02-threshold-and-final-size`) are removed from both trees; the two README indexes still list them and are
rewritten by the later unit.

## Findings for the main session

- `data-pipeline/pipeline/model/holo/methods/baseline/m02_random_superposition.py` documents an intensity spread of about 35 percent
  whatever the grid; the notebook `02-analytic-holograms/02-the-ten-by-ten-benchmark` measures it at 0.1837 uniformity and 40.63 percent
  spread from 128 to 768 pixels and with a flat beam, and states that the published 0.01 and 89 percent are not reproduced. No cause is
  claimed.
- The FFT form and the point-trap form of M04 differ by up to 6e-3 rad at a few pixels where the back-projected SLM field is nearly zero
  (median 3e-5 rad), because the case coordinates are rounded to 0.1 nm and lie 5e-5 bins off their bins. The method docstring says "agree to
  rounding"; the notebook reports the median and the maximum.
