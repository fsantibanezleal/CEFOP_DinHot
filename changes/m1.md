## SDD changes

- Method ladder M17 and B3: the test paths are `tests/learned/test_m17_cgh_net.py` and `tests/learned/test_b3_warm_start.py` (the
  ladder names `tests/methods/...`; the unit's write scope was `tests/learned/`). Repoint the cells or move the files.
- Coordinates of the warmstart-model: `x`, `y` in focal bins (one bin is `lambda f / (pad N p)`), `z` in units of the SLM-limited
  Rayleigh range `z_R = lambda f^2 / (pi R^2)`, `R = N p / 2` (the quadratic delay at the aperture edge is then exactly `Z` radians
  and the forward model is independent of lambda, f and p). Weights are target intensities; a vortex trap is refused.
- B3 default `n_fix = 12` (M07's). The trained candidate with `n_fix = 3` was measured and rejected (report D3).
- Accuracy definition of M17 (DeepCGH's own formula was not read): `AC = 2 sum(T I) / (sum T^2 + sum I^2)`, unit-sum intensities.
- Resources (measured): the training record `data/derived/models/<slot>-training.json` holds the configuration and the sizes.
