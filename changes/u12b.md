# U12b fragment: notebooks of topics 05 to 07 (for the later unit that writes the notebook READMEs and the root README)

Fourteen notebooks per language, the same relative paths in `notebooks/en/` and `notebooks/es/`, topics 05 (optimization), 06 (device and
aberrations) and 07 (feedback and dynamics). Every notebook runs the product's methods through the pipeline modules of `product.json`
`method_entrypoints` (DinHot publishes no package), on cases of `data/examples/cases.json`. The static gate is
`scripts/check_notebooks.py`, the runner is `scripts/run_notebooks.py`, the row tests are `tests/notebooks/test_u12b.py`. The template's
example notebooks are removed by unit U12a, not by this unit.

The unit follows the method tiers of template 0.13.000: each `sota` row of the SDD ladder that belongs to it is named in `caos.methods` by
one level 3 notebook (M10, M11, M12, M13, M16; M07, M08, M17 and M18 belong to U12a and the learned-model unit), and each claim BS-2, BS-4
and BS-5 by one level 4 notebook, in both languages.

## Findings for the main session

- `TrapCost` (the cost and gradient class that notebooks 05-01 to 05-04 use) lives in `methods/optim/_common.py`, a module whose docstring
  calls it shared and private, and is not in `product.json` `method_entrypoints`. The notebooks import it by name; adding it to the
  entrypoints, or to a public module, would make the import non-private.
- `b4_robust.ensemble_stats`, `b2_flicker_bounded.transient_min_ratio` and `.trajectory`, `m16_rearrangement.assign` and `.count_crossings`,
  `b5_pareto.hypervolume` are public functions that are not in `method_entrypoints`; the notebooks use them for scoring and do not count
  them as method cells.
- M13 on the C08 device, started from the weighted-GS hologram, stops after three iterations whatever the budget (5 or 60), with the same
  result; the cause was not isolated (a straight-through quantizer gradient that is not the derivative of the quantized cost is one candidate).
- The cost `C_2` of M10 is not the uniformity: refining the 30-iteration M06 hologram of C12 with it lowers the cost and raises the relative
  standard deviation (`05-optimization/03-conjugate-gradient-against-weighted-gs`).
- B5's front is a property of its starting hologram and budget: M06 at 100 iterations dominates 7 of the 20 points of the default front
  (`05-optimization/05-the-efficiency-uniformity-front`); the SDD's hypervolume criterion holds at the benchmark's 30-iteration baselines.
- B4's gain over M07 falls from 1.60 to 1.14 on a residual 1.5 times wider than the 0.02 waves per mode it was designed for, and the pointing
  ratio is 1.01 (`06-device-and-aberrations/04-robust-holograms`).
- On the 6-by-6 sort of case C10 the exponent 1.5 of M16 leaves one pair of crossing paths (the paper's guarantee is for a square lattice
  and exponents above about 1.12); exponents 1.0, 1.2, 2.0 and 3.0 leave none (`07-feedback-and-dynamics/02-assigning-atoms-to-sites`).
- B2's claim (minimum transient intensity of a moving trap at least 0.9 of its mean) holds on its 4-by-4 acceptance task with moves of 4
  bins and fails on the long moves of C10 unless the end phases are given and the frames are doubled
  (`07-feedback-and-dynamics/05-flicker-bounded-moves`).
- In the device model a fill factor below 1 flattens the pixel envelope and charges no power for the dead area (the module says so), so a fill
  factor of 0.85 slightly improves the spread and the efficiency of C08 (`06-device-and-aberrations/01-what-the-device-costs`).
